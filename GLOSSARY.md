# Housebook

A shared record of everything about one house: what is in it, the documents that go with those things, and the work done on them.

## Language

**House**:
The single home a Housebook records.
_Avoid_: Home, property, residence

**Household**:
The people who share one House and its Housebook.
_Avoid_: Family, account, team

**Member**:
One person in the Household. Every Member has the same access.
_Avoid_: User, owner, admin

**Invite**:
A one-time link a Member creates and shares so that a new person can join the Household.
_Avoid_: Invitation code, join link

**Item**:
A physical thing the Household must look after, whether part of the House (a roof, a furnace) or owned for it (a lawn mower).
_Avoid_: Asset, appliance, thing

**Retired**:
An Item no longer in the House that keeps its history and Documents, such as a replaced water heater.
_Avoid_: Deleted, removed, inactive

**Document**:
A file that belongs to exactly one Item or one Service Record, such as a manual, warranty, or receipt.
_Avoid_: Attachment, file, paperwork

**Service Record**:
One visit or job of work on the House, covering zero or more Items; zero means the whole House.
_Avoid_: Maintenance entry, log entry, job, visit

**Maintenance Log**:
The list of all Service Records for the House.
_Avoid_: History, timeline

**Contractor**:
A person or company outside the Household who does work on the House.
_Avoid_: Vendor, provider, tradesperson

**Archived**:
A Contractor the Household no longer uses, kept on past Service Records.
_Avoid_: Deleted, inactive
